[English report](README.md) · [한국어 보고서](README.ko.md)

All 108 PNG/SVG curve pairs below are original server evaluation outputs. The current full-4040 and historical holdout runs are listed separately; they are not one comparison cohort. No curves were regenerated during this review.

Each curve belongs to the dataset and variant in its path. `pr` plots mean precision against mean recall; `fbeta` plots mean F-beta (beta squared = 0.3) against uint8 threshold 0–255; `emeasure` plots mean E-measure against the same thresholds. The blue line is the dataset-level curve, with no uncertainty band. These are metric plots, not spatial overlays.

The generating source is [fsa_study/metrics.py](../../fsa_study/metrics.py). Original threshold CSVs and source/artifact hashes are retained alongside the plots and in [archive-audit.json](archive-audit.json), [artifact-audit.json](artifact-audit.json), and each evaluation metrics.json. The [verified original ZIP](https://github.com/sh-min/INS_COD/releases/tag/server-transfer-20261102) also preserves all 54,995 prediction PNGs.
